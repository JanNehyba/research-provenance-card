# Roles Vocabulary (CRediT + RPC Extensions)

## CRediT Roles
RPC uses CRediT role identifiers with the `credit:` prefix (e.g., `credit:conceptualization`, `credit:writing_original_draft`).

## RPC AI Extension Roles
- `rpc:source_retrieval`
- `rpc:text_generation`
- `rpc:code_generation`
- `rpc:prompt_orchestration`
- `rpc:agent_operation`

## Mapping Note
This vocabulary is designed to remain mappable to future standard disclosures (including potential Vancouver-aligned taxonomies).
